# Third-party geometry

These notices cover the source assets in `cad/` and the corresponding geometry in the Blender scenes and previews. STEP files are redistributed under their upstream licences. The `.mesh.json` files are tessellations of the STEP geometry. The generator places, orients and colors those meshes for the Moby assembly.

The U1 ESP32-S3 board and M3 amplifier in the public scenes are generated by Moby's source code from mechanical dimensions and pin layouts. See the [component table](moby-mini-components.md) for the hardware references.
